# Math Workshop — Design Contract

## World

A proof sheet on a quiet study desk: blue construction ink, vermilion annotations, a warm paper field, and one diagram surface where measurement lines are literal evidence. It is a visual reasoning artifact, not a gamified course shell.

## System

- Palette: ink `#18243a`, construction blue `#2454a5`, vermilion `#cc5548`, warm paper `#f4f1e8`, yellow margin note.
- Typography: Georgia gives the question and equations a patient editorial voice; Courier New labels coordinates, steps, and sheet metadata.
- Composition: problem index, one proof sheet, one diagram, and a vertical sequence of reasoning moves. The layout follows the proof, not a card catalog.
- Interaction: selecting a problem resets the proof; each reveal advances one step and exposes the reason; reset returns the sheet to its folded state.
- Responsive: index precedes the proof sheet; the diagram and givens stack on phones; the question remains the dominant reading anchor.

## Motion

Proof steps reveal discretely through state and content, with no decorative animation. The construction grid exists only inside the diagram surface where it carries measurement meaning.
