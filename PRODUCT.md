# Math Workshop

Math Workshop is a visual proof desk for unpacking one mathematical idea at a time. The first surface should make the selected problem, its diagram-like quantities, and the next proof step legible while giving the learner a deliberate reveal interaction.

## Audience and scene

A student or self-directed learner working through a hard problem on a quiet desk. Content is illustrative and synthetic; the interface is a learning artifact, not a calculator or assessment system.

## Success

The learner chooses a problem, reads the givens, reveals proof steps in order, and can restart the exploration without losing the visual relationship between quantities.

## Boundaries

- Keep the product self-contained and client-side.
- Include three synthetic problems with honest, readable notation.
- Make problem selection, step reveal, and reset controls work.
- Avoid generic card-grid course layouts and progress-badge gamification.
